About this log

I'm spending nine months teaching English in Kudus, on the north coast of Central Java. This is a place to keep a loose, public record of that — short posts, a photo or two, whatever seems worth writing down.

This isn't the whole story. Some of what I'm thinking through stays in a private notebook — this site is the part I'm comfortable sharing as it happens.

Built as a plain static site, no platform, no tracking. If you want to follow along without checking back, the RSS feed works in any feed reader.
